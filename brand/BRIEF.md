# Brand brief: Ridgeline (Rwanda)

**Name switched.** The assigned name was **Thousand Hills**, but two companies already trade under it in Rwanda: **1000 Hills Safaris**, a Kigali tour company selling customised Rwanda and East Africa safaris (SafariBookings lists it as founded in 2015), and **Thousand Hills Africa**, a Rwandan ground operator now folded into **Wilderness**, which is this brand's primary design reference (it became Wilderness Rwanda's travel-shop and touring business). Using the name would look like a copy of both. This brief uses the best runner-up, **Ridgeline**. The owner should confirm.

Slug and repo: `rwanda`. Country: Rwanda (RW). Brand type: country. Brief v1, 2026-10-26. Facts below were checked on that date. Anything marked *verify* needs an official source before launch.

## 1. Name

**Ridgeline** (recommended). Rwanda is a country of ridges. Villages, churches, tea estates and markets run along the tops of the hills, with terraces cut down the sides. Its spine is the Congo-Nile Divide: rain on the western side runs to Lake Kivu and the Congo, and rain on the eastern side runs to the Nile. "Ridgeline" keeps the idea of "Thousand Hills" in one plain English word that is easy to say, spell and remember. It also describes how the brand plans: along the country's high lines, from the Virunga volcanoes to Nyungwe, not just one hour with the gorillas.
- Search check: one web search (2026-10-26) found no Rwanda tour company called Ridgeline. The results were other Kigali operators (Silverback Africa, Rwanda Eco Company, Hermosa Life).
- Trademark: a human must search the **Rwanda Development Board's intellectual property registry** (https://org.rdb.rw or the RDB IP office, classes 39 and 43), plus **UKIPO**, **EUIPO** and **USPTO**. The domain and social handles are also a human task.
- Risks: **Honda Ridgeline** is a pickup truck (class 12, not travel, but it dominates search). "Ridgeline" is used by US outdoor and real-estate businesses. Search ads need negative keywords.

Runners-up:
1. **Twin Lakes**: Burera and Ruhondo below the volcanoes. Plain English and calm, but generic (dozens of US places and resorts share it), so it is hard to own.
2. **Golden Monkey**: the Virunga primate most visitors meet after the gorillas. Memorable and warm, but it pulls in Chinese snub-nosed monkeys and a restaurant chain in search.
3. **Kivu**: the great western lake, short and easy to say. Risk: the lake is shared with DRC, and the North and South Kivu provinces carry conflict news.

Rejected: **Thousand Hills** (assigned; taken, see above) and **Silverback** (Silverback Africa already operates from Kigali).

## 2. Positioning

*For travellers who want more than one hour with the gorillas: Rwanda planned along its ridges, from the volcanoes to Nyungwe, Kivu and Akagera, at walking pace and with Kigali's context.*

The gap. Rwanda's top end is sold through its lodges: Wilderness Bisate, Singita Kwitonda and One&Only Gorilla's Nest and Nyungwe House. UK specialists (Scott Dunn, Yellow Zebra and others) mostly package Rwanda as a two- or three-night gorilla add-on, or as "gorillas plus Akagera by helicopter". Nobody owns a **single-country, whole-Rwanda** edit: the forest, the lake and the savannah given proper time, the culture of the south and east (Nyanza, Huye, imigongo), and Kigali's memorial and its art scene handled with care.

## 3. Audience

**A. The primate-and-more couple (UK/US, 50s–60s, 9–12 nights).**
- Motivation: the gorillas first, then chimps in Nyungwe, Big Five in Akagera, and real time on the lake. Wants to understand the country, not just pass through.
- Objections: permit cost and fitness; worries about the DRC border, Ebola and the genocide's legacy; "Is Rwanda enough for 10 days?"

**B. The returning East Africa traveller (US/Europe, 35–55, 6–8 nights, often after Kenya or Tanzania).**
- Motivation: has done the Mara. Wants forest, walking, design-led lodges and Kigali's food and galleries.
- Objections: "Can't the lodge just book this?" Needs proof that a specialist improves permits, guides and pacing.

## 4. Brand story (≈190 words)

> Stand on the Congo-Nile Divide in Nyungwe after rain and you can watch the water choose. On one side it runs down to Lake Kivu and, in the end, the Congo. On the other it starts the long journey to the Nile. Rwanda is built on lines like that one: ridges with a church and a market on top, terraces of tea and beans cut down their sides, mist lying in the valleys until mid-morning.
>
> We named the company after the ridgeline because it is how Rwanda is lived and how we plan it. Most visitors fly in for one hour with the mountain gorillas and leave. We plan along the high ground instead: the bamboo slopes of the Virunga volcanoes, Nyungwe's canopy, the slow shore of Lake Kivu, and the lower plains of Akagera, where lions and rhino have returned.
>
> We start in Kigali, at the memorial, because the country asks you to. We book the trackers, the coffee washing station and the women painting imigongo in black, ochre and white. And we leave an afternoon on a ridge with nothing scheduled.

## 5. Voice

1. **Respectful.** Rwanda's history is present; treat it with care and never as a hook.
   - Do: "We begin at the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Allow a morning. We don't plan anything loud afterwards."
   - Don't: "Discover Rwanda's dark past and inspiring rebirth!"
2. **Unhurried.** Give each place its nights and say why.
   - Do: "Two nights at Volcanoes so a weather delay doesn't cost you the trek."
   - Don't: "Gorillas in a day: fly in, trek, fly out!"
3. **Precise.** Altitude, hours, fitness, rules. Facts the traveller can plan with.
   - Do: "Treks start around 2,500 m and can take from one hour to most of the day."
   - Don't: "An epic jungle adventure into the mist."
- Banned: Land of a Thousand Hills (as a tagline), Switzerland of Africa, Africa's success story, dark tourism, gorillas in the mist (as a pun), selfie, hidden gem, breathtaking, unforgettable, bucket list, once in a lifetime, "untouched", and "tribe".

Sample headlines:
- One hour with the gorillas. Ten days with the country.
- The rain decides on the ridge: Congo or Nile.
- Nyungwe's canopy, then the lake.
- Akagera: savannah at the bottom of the hills.
- Kigali first, and slowly.

## 6. Palette, fonts, design

Lead pairing: **imigongo black + ochre-red geometric + cream**, after the cow-dung relief panels of eastern Rwanda. The closest taken lead is "red earth + indigo": Ridgeline has no blue at all, and its red is a burnt ochre used as geometry, not a ground. Batch siblings are distinct: Uganda (Crested Crane) is grey, gold and papyrus green; Malawi (Lantern Lake) is cichlid blue and lantern orange.

All seasons are *verify* against Meteo Rwanda.

**Two signature features:**
- **The Ridge (`/the-ridge/`).** A west-to-east elevation profile across Rwanda, from Lake Kivu over the Congo-Nile Divide and Nyungwe to Kigali and Akagera, with a second north profile up to the Virunga volcanoes. The profile is pre-sampled at build time from public-domain SRTM data (or verified spot heights) stored in `ridge.json`. Each stop shows altitude, what the air feels like, what to pack, and what the trek or walk asks. A droplet animation splits at the divide, Congo one way, Nile the other.
- **The Rwandan Calendar (`/calendar/`).** The year as a visitor should know it, computed for the next 18 months: Umuganda (last Saturday of the month, morning community work, when traffic and many services pause), Kwibuka (from 7 April, the commemoration period), public holidays, Kwita Izina (the gorilla naming, date announced yearly), and the long and short rains. The planner warns when an arrival or long transfer falls on an Umuganda morning and suggests a change.

## 9. Licensing route (human decision)

- **In Rwanda:** tour operators and travel agencies must hold a tourism licence from the **Rwanda Development Board** (https://rdb.rw), and gorilla permits are booked through RDB by licensed operators. The practical route is to contract a licensed Rwandan operator and record its RDB licence. Membership of the Rwanda Tours and Travel Association is voluntary.
- **Selling to UK customers through a UK company:** ATOL for flight-inclusive packages (https://www.caa.co.uk/atol-protection/) plus insolvency protection under the Package Travel Regulations 2018.
- **US:** check seller-of-travel registration for California, Florida, Washington and Hawaii.

## 10. Top 3 risks

1. **The DRC border and Ebola.** FCDO advises against all but essential travel within 10km of the DRC border in Rusizi District, including the Lake Kivu islands and **Kamembe Airport**: so no flights into Kamembe for Nyungwe, no southern Kivu stays, and access to Nyungwe by road from Huye or Kigali. Rubavu faces Goma across the border: watch the advice before every booking. Ebola screening is in place and could change at short notice.
2. **Ethics and tone.** The genocide memorials, the gorillas and the Batwa all need care: no dark-tourism framing, memorial photography only where allowed, primate rules (distance, masks if required) stated plainly, imigongo licensed from its makers.
3. **The name and the market.** The assigned name was taken; Ridgeline is unchecked with RDB IP and UKIPO, and "Honda Ridgeline" crowds search. The lodges (Wilderness, Singita, One&Only) sell direct, so the whole-country edit must show on every page.
